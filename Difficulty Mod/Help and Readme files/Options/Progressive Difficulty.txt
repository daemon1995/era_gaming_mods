Progressive Difficulty
======================

What it does
Raises AI difficulty later in the game. Active progression dates are displayed in the current-settings summary.

How to use it
Enable and enter the progression day. It cannot precede Payday; an earlier entry is corrected to Payday with a message. Available presets may also include a second increase. Insane cannot be raised beyond the normal maximum.

Purpose
Allow a manageable opening followed by stronger opposition later.
