Neutral Exp Gain
================

What it does
Controls neutral creature stack-experience progression. Ace gives rank 10 from day 1 instead of waiting for gradual progression. This is creature experience, not hero battle experience.

How to use it
The checkbox applies the selected rank as a minimum for AI creature stacks in battle. Higher existing experience and partial progress are preserved; normal AI experience gain is not reduced. Commanders and war machines are excluded.

Purpose
Keep creature abilities relevant as the game progresses and help AI armies whose stacks may not naturally reach high ranks.
