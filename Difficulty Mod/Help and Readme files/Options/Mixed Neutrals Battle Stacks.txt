Mixed Neutrals Battle Stacks
============================

What it does
Click the label or value to cycle forward. The arrows cycle in either direction.

Classic: original Min/Max Mixing and Stack Spread rules.
Few stacks: 30 / 25 / 20 / 15 / 5 / 3 / 2%.
Balanced: 10 / 15 / 20 / 20 / 15 / 10 / 10%.
Many stacks: 2 / 3 / 5 / 15 / 20 / 25 / 30%.

The seven percentages refer to 1 through 7 battle stacks.

Custom JSON: uses enabled, chance1 to chance7 and per-creature overrides from mixed neutrals stack count.json in the Mixed Neutrals mod's Lang folder. Edit these weights as desired. Fixed presets ignore those JSON values.

Min/Max Mixing still controls the initial mixture, capped by the selected battle stack count. Splitting preserves its total fighting value. Small armies may produce fewer stacks. Existing custom army templates take priority. Stack Spread no longer fills empty slots to seven when a stack count is selected.

Confirm saves the choice; Cancel discards changes. Applies when a new map is initialized, not to existing armies. Restart the game after editing JSON.

How to use it
Select Classic, Few stacks, Balanced, Many stacks or Custom JSON. Confirm saves; Cancel discards changes.

Purpose
Control how a neutral army is distributed across battle stacks without simply filling every battle with seven stacks.
