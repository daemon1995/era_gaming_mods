AI Difficulty and Payday
========================

What it does
AI difficulty selects the strength of computer-player bonuses. Payday sets when the timed AI bonuses begin.

How to use it
Choose the difficulty and enter Payday when confirming AI settings. A later Payday gives human players more time to develop. Start with a moderate difficulty; high settings can stack with the other AI bonuses.

Purpose
Separate the eventual strength of the computer player from the time at which that pressure begins.
