Battle Reward
=============

What it does
Enables the mod's additional battle-reward system. The calculated chance depends on selected difficulty options, including Neutral Start Bonus and reduced Battle Experience.

How to use it
Enable manually. Relevant setting changes show the current chance while enabled; otherwise normal hints are displayed. The three difficulty portraits do not toggle this option.

Purpose
Provide an optional incentive for harder battles without forcing additional rewards on players who only want more difficulty.
