Low-Level Growth
================

What it does
Adds weekly growth steps by creature level: level 1 +10%, level 2 +5%, level 3 +4%, level 4 +3%, level 5 +2%, levels 6-7 +1%. These are additional growth steps, with integer rounding.

How to use it
Use when weak creatures fall behind too quickly. Higher growth limits control how far stacks can ultimately grow.

Purpose
Help low-level guards remain relevant without giving the same percentage boost to every creature tier.
