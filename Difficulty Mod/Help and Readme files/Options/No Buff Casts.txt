No Buff Casts
=============

What it does
Sets remaining casts to zero for neutral creatures whose active spell is a fixed beneficial buff. This also prevents that creature from buffing allies. Offensive spells, random spell pools, healing and resurrection are excluded.

How to use it
Toggle No Buff Casts. It targets creature spellcasting rather than the separate Buffs option.

Purpose
Some added creatures, particularly in TUM, repeatedly cast buffs instead of attacking and become easier opponents. This option lets those creatures fight instead.
