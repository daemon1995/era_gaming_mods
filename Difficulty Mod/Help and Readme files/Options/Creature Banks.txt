Creature Banks
==============

What it does
Controls the difficulty and growth of creature-bank guards independently of ordinary map stacks.

How to use it
Use its arrows for bank difficulty. The adjacent checkbox separately applies Neutral Stack Size at map start: the additional creatures are distributed among occupied guard stacks. For example, a level-1 bonus of 30 adds 6 to each of five level-1 stacks. Perry/TUM bank templates are supported.

Purpose
Let players tune bank encounters separately and optionally give banks a moderate starting increase.
