Other Mod Settings
==================

What it does
The difficulty window offers configuration entry points for installed companion mods, including Arena, Raid Boss, Bounty Hunter, Stack-Experience Rebalance and Enhanced Henchmen.

How to use it
The corresponding mod must be installed and active. These buttons configure that mod; they do not install it. 

Purpose
Keep related difficulty settings accessible from one place while each companion mod retains its own gameplay.
