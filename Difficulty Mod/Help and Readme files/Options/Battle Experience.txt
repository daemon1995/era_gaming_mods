Battle Experience
=================

What it does
Scales normal hero experience from combat from 10% to 200%, in steps of 10%. Default: 100%. Learning is included. Chests, quests and scripted rewards are unchanged; battles that award no experience still award none.

How to use it
The checkbox extends the selected percentage to AI heroes. Otherwise only human heroes are affected. Lower percentages also affect the Battle Reward calculation.

Purpose
Adjust hero progression independently of enemy strength, especially on maps with many battles.
