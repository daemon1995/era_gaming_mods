Neutral Scaling Bonus
=====================

What it does
Adds Attack, Defense and HP as game time advances. This is separate from Neutral Start Bonus.

How to use it
Select the scaling strength with the arrows. The adjacent checkbox controls speed only: Easy/Normal none; Hard/Extreme +1 from week 13; Insane +1 from week 9; Insane I/II +1 from week 9 and +2 total from week 25.

Purpose
Keep neutral encounters relevant later in the game while allowing players to opt out of the much more influential speed bonus.
