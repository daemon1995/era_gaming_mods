 1. Main features: 

The Advanced Classes Mod is a significant modification that enhances the gameplay of Wake of Gods. At its core, the mod introduces a new class and skill system for Heroes 3. To avoid bugs, nearly the entire WoG mod has been rewritten. This makes ACM very stable. 
It is now possible to play as one of three distinct hero classes (Warrior, Mage or Adventurer), each with their own strengths and weaknesses.

There are new Master and Grandmaster levels for every secondary skill.
- The usefulness of weaker skills has been greatly improved.
- Three main hero classes: Warrior, Mage and Adventurer. 
- Three completely new hybrid classes: Battlemage, Hunter and Druid.
- New unique abilities for every class at the highest level
- Nearly all Hero specialisations have been reworked, including creature, spell and adventurer specialists.
- Reworked primary and secondary skill point chance at level up for each class
- The Magic System has been completely reworked so that now all spells scale with Spell Power.
- New Master and Grandmaster levels for every combat spell, with new effects and powers
- Reworked Commander abilities — they are now much more active in combat and fun to use!
- Nine new base Commander classes and 18 specialisations, each with unique abilities. This makes a total of 27 choices.
- 24 unique Warmachine upgrades can now be purchased in town.
- Most artefacts have been improved and now form part of a set with interesting bonuses.
- Three new secret class sets have been added.
- New settings to increase the difficulty of neutral units and computer players (AI) in the form of the difficulty mod.
- Good compatibility with WoG options


 2. Important information 

The 'Help' and 'Readme' folders contain additional information about heroes, spells and skills. If you are unsure how a skill works, you can look it up there. All in-game options provide additional information when right-clicked in-game.
Right-clicking or left-clicking on the hero portrait on the hero screen will show you important information about your current class points and skills, which determine your hero's development. Use this feature frequently! 
Right-clicking the experience icon on the hero screen opens the bonus list and shows you lots of additional information about hero attributes and commander classes. 


 2.1 Important key shortcuts
 
- Key shortcut for the class table: press "W" on the hero screen.
- Key shortcut for the bonus list: press "E" on the hero screen.
- The key shortcut for the advanced hero class is "A" in the hero screen.
- Key shortcut for spell upgrade list: press "R" in the hero screen. "R" on the hero screen.


 2.2 Brief overview of some important changes to WoG mechanics
 
Heroes:
Most hero specialisations have been reworked and significantly improved in terms of usefulness, especially for magic heroes. There are also some new and unique specialities. Every hero should now be 'mainable'. Speciality scaling per level has decreased from 5% to 3%. Some heroes have had their starting secondary skills changed to better match their speciality. 

Artifacts:
New unique effects have been added to unused and boring artifacts, and new artifact sets have been introduced. Set bonuses no longer require a complete set, however the strongest bonuses still do. The class of several artefacts has also been changed. Don't forget to read the artefact description.

Warmachine:
- The buggy Warmachine scripts (1, 2 and 3) have been cleaned up. There is now only one Warmachine of each type, and the 20 Ballistas nonsense has been eliminated. The functionality of the WoG Warmachine scripts I-III is included, just interpreted in a new way. 
Tents now resurrect in combat, but have limited healing power called 'Heal Capacity'. This is shown in the bonus list and by right-clicking your tent during combat. It is equal to 2% of your total army HP, plus other bonuses such as skills, artefacts and WM upgrades. Ammo carts and ballistas have been improved and can be upgraded in town. You can purchase over 20 unique upgrades for your WM in towns. These are divided into "Enchantments", which can be purchased at the Mage Guild, and "Upgrades", which can be obtained at the Blacksmith. Your hero can only purchase one ultimate upgrade per upgrade/enchantment master. However, these upgrades are difficult to obtain. If your hero has learned more than ten secondary skills, they cannot be promoted any further than level one. This prevents people from abusing the power of the upgrades by playing with 28 skills, and adds replay value.

Spells:
By far the most work went into reworking the magic system. Now, all spells scale with spell power! The strength of your spells depends on your character's Magic Strength (MS), a new primary stat introduced in ACM. Caster heroes are now clearly better spellcasters than a Tazar with Earth Magic. The power of the Slow spell has been reduced and it has been moved to level 3. Spell point costs have increased, especially at expert level. Multicasting in the same battle round will increase spell point costs until the next battle round, which should prevent the use of too many spells in the same round. 

Commanders:
Disabled most of the old WoG commander features and remade them. Commanders now require at least one point in the Magic skill in order to cast spells. Spells have been made much more useful — read the description. All commanders now benefit from carrying out actual attacks in combat; some can even increase their damage or health permanently by killing entire squads.
When you make your first kill in combat, you can choose a commander class. Each class has different attributes that can be viewed by right-clicking the experience icon on the hero screen, then right-clicking the commander class text field.
When you reach a certain kill count with your commander, you can choose a new class that usually specialises in a certain ability.
The mastery of your commander is unlocked when you reach half of your maximum kill count.

Eagle Eye:
Spells are now learned directly in combat, giving you permanent spell power. There are also some other benefits to this skill.

Luck/Morale:
Morale now caps at a maximum of +6. Each point of morale increases your chance of gaining an extra move by ~3% (instead of 5%). Luck is increased to a maximum of +6, but the damage scaling for Luck > 6 is capped at 15%. At some point, only the damage from your luck hits will increase. Check out the new window in the Heroes Screen.

Diplomacy:
- Complete skills reworked. It now gives you a chance to attract creatures from your actual army, rather than the stack found on the adventure map. You can hire them directly for an increased price. This makes Diplomacy more useful and predictable; it is no longer possible for a random free join of thousands of neutrals to give you an instant win. Free joining of stacks from the adventure map is more or less disabled.

Fire Shield:
Returned damage now depends on your Spell Power as well, and can exceed the original damage amount. This gives the spell much more tactical depth. Some creatures are now immune to Fire Shield, such as dragons and other units with a fire affinity. Fire Shields that are passively granted due to SE or other abilities will only return 20% of the damage.   

Summoning Elementals:
The HP of summons now depends on your MS. Sums can also grant buffs to your army while they are alive on the battlefield. Summoning Air is available early, but is therefore very weak. Higher-level summon spells, such as Earth, will have better scaling.

Navigation:
The skill has been deleted and replaced with the Nobility skill. Nobility can increase creature growth in towns once per week. The increased movement on water will still be applied when you select Nobility. 

Creature stats and town lineups:
No changes were made to creatures or towns. I'll leave that to the other mods.


 3. How do you play this mod?
 
Play a normal H3 game, but decide early on which class your hero should reach. If you play the standard eight secondary skill game, you need 35 points to reach Master Class and 58 points to reach Grandmaster Class, but you can always check. 
Each Master skill gives you two class points! You can only learn Master and Grandmaster skills if you are M/GM in that class.
Check your class points often to see how many points you get from secondary skills to reach a certain class. Check your progression/class points after every hero level-up.
Please read the 'Readme' section about AI difficulty before starting the game. It is recommended that you skip the difficulty settings in your first game to get used to the mod. 


 4. Frequently asked questions:
 
Which WoG settings can I use?
Answer: Feel free to use any setting you like. There are no specific ACM settings that need to be activated. Incompatible settings are mostly disabled. If a setting is not working, please report it so that it can be fixed. 

Why is this particular WoG or ERA script option blocked by the Advanced Classes Mod? How can I restore it?
Answer: Browse to Heroes3/Mods/Advanced Classes Mod/Data/s and check the '80 wog - disable options.erm' file. Each option has a brief explanation of why it is disabled. If you want to enable it anyway, you must delete both entries in the file. There are always two.

What damage does my Artillery do? How much will my Tent heal in combat? How much magic pierce does my hero have? What is my total magical critical chance? How can I check the new stats of my commander?
Answer: Right-click on the Experience icon on your hero screen (press "E"). Most numbers are displayed there. Some fields offer more details when you right-click on them, such as the commander class.

Why can't I level up some skills to Master?
Answer: You don't have enough class points to reach the Master level for that skill. Check your current class points and level (press "W").

What happens if I learn a secondary skill and later forget it? Will I keep all bonuses?
Answer: No, when you unlearn a secondary skill, you will lose the M/GM level associated with it and may even drop out of your current class.

Right-click on the Kingdom Overview.
Answer: The Kingdom Overview shows your income, including spending from the previous day. Keep that in mind — the numbers might be negative if you spend more than you earn. Money calculations are done when you click, so do it early in your turn.

There are many new effects on artefacts, spells and skills. How do they scale? I want to do the maths!
Answer: Nearly all new effects have some sort of scaling. Check the information window by right-clicking the experience icon. You can find even more information in the bonus list by right-clicking on the spell section on the left. You should also check out the help and readme files for skills and spells. These are up to date, and you can find formulas there.

During combat, troops deal zero damage. I'd better report that a dozen times in Discord, haha!
Answer: Carefully read your artefact description. Some artefacts and the Druid class have the ability to block a set amount of damage. The amount of damage blocked is small and will be irrelevant at later stages of the game.

What happens if I play with more than 10 secondary skills?
Answer: You can try, but ACM does not support it. Having more than 10 skills makes it much easier to create heroes that belong to several classes at once. It should work overall, but I don't know what will happen. If you want to try, use the ERA scripts option to allow more secondary skills.

I want to learn all secondary skills to Master and Grandmaster regardless of my current class!
Answer: Check out the new game mode made by Berserker. Activate it at map start.

With the Berserker option, I can learn all the skills to Master and Grandmaster regardless of my class, but can I get rid of the whole class system? I don't want to play as a Hunter, Druid, etc.
Answer: Yes, use the Berserker option in combination with the 'Select Class' option. When you are first asked to choose a class, select 'Never ask again' to disable the class system for this hero.

What does Magic Strength do?
Answer: It is a new stat mainly for caster heroes, ensuring they are better at casting than warrior heroes. The power of some spells now depends on your MS rather than your Spell Power. 1 SP equals 1 MS; several secondary skills also give you MS. - The Magic Strength scaling factor is as follows: XXL-Maps 80%, XL-Maps 85%, L-Maps 100%, M-Map 110%, S-Maps 120%. This ensures a similar spell power on all map sizes.

I really like the Spell Trainer option, but I think the damage increase per level is too low. Can this be changed?
Answer: Yes, in the ERM file, search for 'damage increment' and change it from 1% to 5%, for example. Now, each time you level up, you will receive +5% damage for damage spells.

Can I use THIS or THAT mod together with the AC mod?
Answer: Most probably, yes. Just try it out. Usually, skill descriptions are overwritten, but the effect will still work/apply in most cases. 

I have seen pictures of new, cool upgrades and even enchantments for war machines. How can I acquire them?
Answer: For upgrades, right-click the Blacksmith in town; for enchantments, right-click your Mage Guild. 

The game is now too easy/difficult.
Answer: I gave you all the options to adjust the difficulty to your liking. Just check the readme to see what each option does. If your game is too easy, increase the difficulty.

I saw that you increased offensive damage from 30% to 31%, and that Eagle Eye now deals damage before combat. LOL. Also, you disabled my favourite WoG option. ROFL. Isn't your mod completely useless?
Answer: If you're asking these questions, maybe it's time you started modding your own game. This modification is the result of several years of modding Heroes 3 WoG. I tried to keep all the fun mechanics that WoG gave us, but some of them had to be disabled because they were simply overpowered and made it impossible to deliver a coherent mod as a whole. 
Examples include the 'Warmachine scripts', the 'Hero Specialisation script', the 'Commander Abilities', and some 'artifact and secondary skills scripts'. I have rewritten all of these scripts from scratch, toned them down, and added new features that I believe are superior to the originals. If you cannot live without one of the disabled options, you can ask me to "reenable" it. 

LOL, Perry, this Battle Commander option is a joke — I always have to reload, and the Battle Reward option makes the game too easy.
Answer: Well, nobody forces you to use these options. They scale depending on your hero level and army power, so they should never be too strong or too weak. In my opinion, Battle Commander is doable and a nice addition. 
Did you know that you can disable the Battle Commander option during the game by right-clicking the system icon, creating new settings and disabling Battle Commander? After the fight, you can enable it again by creating new settings. Problem solved! 

I activated the 'Growth Beyond 4000' option and now I'm facing 20k peasants that I can't defeat to finish my map. Any ideas?
Answer: Well, well, you're a noob! Right-click the system option icon on the adventure screen. Make new settings. Disable the 'Growth Beyond 4000' option. Fight the 4,000 peasants. After the fight, activate the "Growth Beyond 4000" option again and the problem will be solved.

What is the Warrior class?
Answer: The warrior has a good understanding of combat tactics and strategies, and increases the damage of his troops with his great combat knowledge. He also boosts the morale and luck of his troops. At the highest ranks, his troops will deal significant damage with their attacks.

What is the Mage class?
Answer: The mage boosts the stats of his creatures with strong defence spells. By studying all elements, they gain access to powerful combat spells, which they can cast several times to destroy their enemies. Fight the mage as soon as you can, or he will become unstoppable.

What is the Adventurer class?
Answer: Adventurer: The Adventurer is a swift explorer, and no one is faster than him. They strengthen the hit points of their troops in combat, and with their knowledge of first aid and spiritual magic, they can resurrect their fallen troops after every fight. He is also excellent at managing his kingdom. At grandmaster level, his army will outnumber any other army in Erathia.


Additional information about integrated mods can be found in the mod folder, e.g. changes to Starting Heroes, Neutral and AI Difficulty, Spell Changes, some formulas and more.
Please deactivate all my other 'Enhanced Magic Skills' mods — they are all integrated. 
This mod is intended for use with only eight hero secondary skills. If you want to play with ten, I recommend Igriks's 10 Sec Skills Mod. If you use that, please deactivate the Secondary Skill Scrolling Mod. Playing with 10 skills makes it easier to reach class ranks, but points will be adjusted.
