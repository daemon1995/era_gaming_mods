Your commander can only carry one of each type of Warmachine in their backpack.
Each Warmachine can be upgraded in town by right-clicking on the Blacksmith (Upgrades) or the Mage Guild (Enchantments).

You can view the current upgrades by right-clicking on the Warmachines in the Hero Screen or during combat.

With version 1.095, the mod introduces a new stat called 'Warmachine Efficiency'. This new modifier increases the following parameters:
- Ballista damage (e.g. if your WM efficiency is 150%, your damage will be 50% higher than before).
- the damage of the first ballista shot
- healing of the first aid tent during combat
- the healing power of the first aid tent during combat; and
- the amount of spell points regenerated by your ammo cart (+1 SP for 20% efficiency).
- the health points of all your war machines
- the damage caused by your castle's arrow towers
- the damage of the Magic Artillery perk, provided by the Eagle Eye secondary skill

There are several sources from which to obtain this stat. The main sources are Warmachine upgrades at levels 2 and 3.

First Aid Tent:
First Aid tents now resurrect during and after combat, but have limited healing power called 'Heal Capacity'. This is shown in the bonus list and by right-clicking your tent during combat. It is equal to 2% of your total army HP, plus any other bonuses from skills, artefacts and WM upgrades. This ensures that you cannot prolong a fight until all your units are fully recovered, which was a common occurrence in WoG.

The damage of the Ballista scales with your hero's attack, multiplied by the Ballista's base damage. Therefore, skills and artefacts that increase the base damage are very valuable for increasing your damage output.

The Ammo Cart provides your troops with unlimited shots.
It also restores some spell points after combat and increases the damage of certain secondary skill effects. Right-click on the hero backpack for more information.
